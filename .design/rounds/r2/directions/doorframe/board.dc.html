The household tally. Kept days are chalk strokes on the slate of a home you are keeping; the wall keeps every mark ever made, so a run that ends is history, never erasure. A spent day lies flat — still a mark, not a hole.
Signature — THE GATE STROKE: the fifth, diagonal stroke closing a bundle of five kept days. Encodes run length (gates accumulate as the run grows); second channel is geometry (diagonal vs standing); recurs on Today, Calendar, Stats, the paywall and both widget families.
