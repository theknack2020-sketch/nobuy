The tint-mode answer: Escapement's hues die on the Lock Screen, so the accessories are designed hue-blind from the start — meaning lives in arc length (day progress), the terminal GAP + hollow stub (unanswered), the seated-hand glyph (answered), and filled / block / hollow silhouettes (kept / spent / frozen). Colour is a courtesy the Home Screen adds back. The five-truths answer: widgets carry four forms; mandatory-only is deliberately conflated with kept (it keeps the run — the widget reports the run) and the conflation is stated here, not hidden. The dial-vs-number answer: at 76pt the count wins the centre; the face survives as bezel + index + day arc only — no numerals, no rim hand at accessory size. Widgets are free — nothing in any frame advertises, teases, or locks. Background: .containerBackground(.fill.tertiary) everywhere; the frames below sit on system material, never a hardcoded fill. No animation exists in any widget — the beat (the hand seating) lives only in the app; here the answer is a state swap between renders.

One answer or two — decided: Home families offer BOTH. Offering only "No" flatters the record into a lie; two 72×28pt buttons meet the 44pt rule via the widget's padded hit areas and read at arm's length. The Lock Screen families are read-only by decision — tapping deep-links to Today's question. Reason: accessories have no confirmation, no undo, and live on the surface where accidental touches happen (pockets, bedside); a mistaken one-tap answer there writes a false day into a record whose whole value is honesty. The mistake-path that remains on Home is caught in words: the pending frame names the date being written, and the answered frame's third slot says where and until when it can be amended.

PLATFORM RULES THE DRAWINGS RESPECT
· Background is .fill.tertiary — every frame above sits on system material (light home, dark home, Lock, StandBy, Liquid Glass) and owns no fill of its own.
· No animation: the beat exists only in the app. The widget's "moment of the answer" is the pending frame, then the answered render — two honest stills.
· Refresh is budgeted: no live countdown anywhere; every remainder is rounded and prefixed "~" so a stale render cannot lie ("~tonight", "~2 h left").
· Free tier: the whole family ships free; no frame carries Pro wording, price, or lock — first-run shows the question, never a locked state.
· Timeline: entries at the quarter-hour + one at 21:30 + one at midnight (the rollover swaps open→new day).
