# Brand — NoBuy
Name (fixed wording): "NoBuy: No Spend Day Tracker". Identity around it: the seated hand.

## Marks (assets/)
- wordmark.svg — SF Pro Display Bold, tracking −3, with the index tick seated over the "o": the one place type carries the horological mark.
- lockup-horizontal.svg — night tile (icon concept 2 geometry) + wordmark. Primary lockup.
- lockup-stacked.svg — for square placements.
- lockup-single-ink.svg — one-colour version for print/press; any single ink, positive or reversed.

## Clear space & minimums (measured)
- Clear space: x = the index-triangle height. Keep >= 1x on all sides of any mark.
- Wordmark minimum: 88pt wide (below that the index tick closes up — measured at 72pt, failed).
- Lockup tile minimum: 24pt. Icon-only minimum: 18pt (Settings row scale).

## Where it appears
App Store · onboarding screen 1 · Settings ▸ About · press.
## Where it must NEVER appear
Inside Today, Calendar or Stats (the record is the brand there) · on the paywall (nothing sells the seller) · watermarking exports · in notifications (the reminder is a sentence, not a logo).
