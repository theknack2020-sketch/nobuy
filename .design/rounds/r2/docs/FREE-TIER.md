# Free tier map
## Free, forever
- The nightly question, the answer, the run and best counts.
- The freeze — one per month.
- Both interventions: the 10-minute urge surf and the impulse checklist (including custom questions).
- The waiting list, up to 3 held items at once.
- The record's latest 90 days, on every surface.
- The ENTIRE widget family — Home small + medium, Lock circular + rectangular, with the interactive answer (round-2 addendum: the shortest path to the nightly answer is the product's own loop; gating it would gate the promise).
- One dial finish (silver), light and dark.
- Export (CSV) and erase — the user's data is never hostage.

## Why those
The daily loop must be complete and generous free, or the product is a demo, not a tool: the promise ("stop buying things you won't remember") is kept by the free tier. Pro sells DEPTH of the same record — never the record itself, and never the path to it (widgets are free).

## Pro ($4.99 monthly / $39.99 yearly, 7-day trial; prices always from StoreKit)
Every day on record back to the first · five dial finishes · an open (unlimited) waiting list ·
the analysis layer (monthly trend, weekday pattern, streak history, category split, no-spend
rate, savings estimate) · self-set challenges · unlimited essential categories · unlimited
streak freezes.

> **Owner ruling 2026-08-12, applied after round 2.** The round drew Pro as three rows. The
> owner widened it to the list above: three rows is a thin offer at $4.99/month, and the depth
> layer (charts, challenges, categories, freezes) is exactly the kind of thing that keeps
> earning its price month after month, which is what an auto-renewable has to do. The FREE tier
> below is unchanged from the round — the 90-day window and the three waiting slots stay,
> because the paywall's strongest moment is built on them ("you turned the record back past 90
> days; yours holds 143") and CSV export being free keeps the data honestly un-hostage.
> The paywall's standing slot is unchanged; only the Pro list beneath it grows.

## What free NEVER does
Never expires or deletes data · never shows ads · never nags (the paywall opens only at a real limit or from Settings) · never phones home — there is nothing to phone.

## The 60 lifetime owners
Own everything in Pro permanently; never see the paywall; their standing is the first section of Settings, titled "Early supporter".
