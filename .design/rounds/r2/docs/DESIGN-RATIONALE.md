# Design rationale — round 2, Escapement
## Why this direction
Chosen by the owner from round 1 (see docs/ROUND1_DIRECTIONS.md for the full spread and verdict). It earns the taste axis because the product's material IS waiting: ten minutes of urge, twenty-four hours of a want, one day closing at midnight — an escapement is the machine that measures exactly that.

## The three corrections, answered
1. **Calendar re-solved** — the ring is out of the month room. The timing sheet: week-row grid, day cells in the tick vocabulary, a per-week kept-rate rail; the sweep survives only as the header month bezel. Rows compare; arcs don't. The ring keeps the rooms where a single interval is the subject: Today, urge, circular widget. (screens/calendar.dc.html)
2. **Horological distance** — what a user sees that Activity never shows: printed hour numerals, a 15° tick pitch with majors and minors, a midnight index triangle, a rim hand that travels and SEATS, a bezel line, and the remainder always printed in words. Stated on the dial anatomy card (components/inventory.dc.html) and visible on every dial.
3. **Density vs one decision** — the question is the only element on dial-white; every wait lives in the recessed well at reduced scale; in the densest honest state (timer running, two waits, freeze used, run 23, 2 logs) the well gains one fixed row and nothing grows. (screens/today.dc.html, DENSE)

## What crossed from a rejected candidate (M-09)
From 1a Doorframe, one MEASURE: "the wall keeps every mark." Carried as a rule, drawn in Stats: ended runs are permanent settled intervals on the same geometry; nothing zeroes, the current run is merely the one with an open end. No chalk, no slate, no appearance crossed.

## Portfolio arbitration (M-11)
Register occupied: light silvered horology with lume nightfall. Distances kept: not a dark instrument cockpit (RangePilot/COS — this is light-primary, domestic); not porcelain-cobalt craft (Coneglow — no gloss/matte channel, no cobalt job); not water (Citora), settle (Saponora), crumb (Ovenspring), folio (Ethiopian Bible), enamel (KoalaHood). Counts avoid Citora's Ultralight: nothing below weight 500.

## Brave choice per screen (M-10)
- Today: the hand — a clock, not a ring; the question owns the only white.
- Calendar: pre-record days get bare numerals, no cell — "never asked" is not a gap; the kept-rate rail.
- Stats: no charts at all; intervals only; ended runs drawn permanent.
- Settings: the privacy promise is a printed row, not a link; destruction has no colour.
- Onboarding: the last screen IS the product — day one answered inside onboarding.
- Paywall: the standing gauge draws the limit on the user's own record; free window labelled "free, always" above the price.
- Checklist: "Buy it anyway" is a plain word — the tool takes sides only in aggregate.
- Urge surf: the sweep is wait-grey, not accent — a wait is neither good nor bad; ending early is always present.

## Deliberate absences — do not "fix"
No confetti, glow, particles, mascots. No green-good/red-bad. No charts on Today or anywhere (intervals are not charts). No comparison grid, lock, crown, countdown, strikethrough. No fifth tab. No account/cloud/bank/analytics UI. No emoji, no exclamation marks. No sound design. No streak-guilt copy anywhere — the broken-run message reads: "The run ended at 23. It stays in the record. Tonight starts the next one." (Reduce Motion never means "nothing happens": every beat has a written substitute.)

## The five states, per screen — honest mappings
Where a canonical state cannot exist, the mapping is declared on the board: Stats error = N/A (derived locally; failure surfaces on Calendar); Today loading = the midnight rollover; paywall dense = the subscriber sheet. Dense is drawn at the cap everywhere content can grow (calendar logs ×N, stats 26 runs, checklist 7+ questions, waits at slot cap).
