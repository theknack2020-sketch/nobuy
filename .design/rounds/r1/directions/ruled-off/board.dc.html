Ruled Off
NEUTRAL-PREMIUM INSTRUMENT · LIGHT-PRIMARY
The account book, redressed as an instrument. The mission sentence made literal: answering at night is ruling off the day's entry, the way a bookkeeper draws a double rule under a settled total. Absence is the heaviest thing on the page — a kept day is written in ink, and the good colour is ink itself. No green, no red, no cards: hairlines, tabular figures, one value step of paper.
Signature — THE RULING: a single hairline means open; a double ink rule means settled. Encodes closure of the record (day, month, run) — as the run grows, more of the record stands double-ruled; second channel is line count and geometry, never colour; recurs on Today, the calendar month foot, Stats, the paywall standing figure and both widget families.

Accents: ink = the kept truth (weight is the accent, not hue) · oxblood = the spent fact, adult and unalarmed · slate = mandatory, frozen, unanswered.
Themes: five inks — black, iron-gall blue, sepia, drafting green, violet-grey. The ink re-tints; paper and oxblood never move.
Type: SF Pro only. Heavy tabular figures; letterspaced small-caps chrome; no serif, no mono.
