THEMATIC-INSTRUMENT · LIGHT-PRIMARY
Waiting made mechanical. The product's whole mechanism is the interval — ten minutes for a craving, twenty-four hours for a wanted thing, one day closing at midnight — so every interval is the same sweep on the same silvered geometry. By day, blued steel on a silvered face; at night the same face read by lume. Dark is not an inversion, it is nightfall.
Signature — THE SWEEP: the arc of elapsed waiting. Encodes the elapsed fraction of a named wait (salience rises monotonically as the arc closes); second channel is angular position against ticks plus the numeric remainder; recurs on the day dial, the urge timer, waiting-list bezels, the calendar ring and the circular Lock Screen widget.
